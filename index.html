How it works

  1. Speak or type. Voice uses your browser's speech recognition (Chrome, Edge). No account needed.
  2. The plan reader splits the dictation into stages, services, teeth (FDI), quantities, prices, discounts and programmes, and maps every service to the 208-item price list.
  3. Rule checks catch what doesn't add up: lower-jaw sinus lifts, more crowns than implants, conflicting prices, missing implant systems, “with discount” with no amount.
  4. The pricing engine adds everything up. Your answers update the plan and regenerate the PDF as a new version with a change log.
